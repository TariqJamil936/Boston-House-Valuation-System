Project Pipeline

Problem Identification

Objective: Predict median house values (MEDV) to assist real estate stakeholders. Solves the problem of manual valuation bias.

Data Sourcing & Ingestion

Source: UCI/Kaggle Boston Housing Dataset. 506 rows, 13 features. Loaded via Pandas batch ingestion.

Pre-Processing

Imputation: Median strategy used to handle missing values in CRIM, ZN, LSTAT. Data split 80/20 for training/testing.

Modeling (Random Forest)

Selected for non-linear capability and robustness to outliers. Trained with 100 estimators.

Why Random Forest?

  • **Non-Linearity:** Handles complex relationships between rooms, pollution, and price better than linear models.
  • **Outlier Robustness:** The "committee" of trees prevents single outliers (like the $10M mansion) from skewing results.
  • **No Scaling Needed:** Decision trees do not require Normalization or Standardization.
